How the model works

  • Steps are 0.4 m deep. Standers leave 0.75–1.1 m to the person in front (roughly one empty step); on "every step" they leave 0.42 m.
  • Walkers climb 0.4–0.9 m/s on top of the belt and need 1.1 m of clear space. They tire on long escalators, losing up to half their climbing speed by 60 m.
  • Up to four people at the front of the crowd can step on, so someone heading for a free side can slip past someone waiting for the busy one.
  • A walker who catches up with someone slower turns red and moves at their pace. Nobody overtakes on the same side.
  • Changing crowd size, length, walker share or a scenario restarts the race. Belt speed, arrivals and sim speed apply live.
